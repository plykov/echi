# Echo Loop — Dutch Starter Set

## B1 conversation extension

The additional 100 B1 lessons are available in the app as a dedicated B1 filter and a three-item conversation sprint. They cover routines, work, home, travel, social repair, health, opinions, learning, practical problem-solving, and community life. Every item has an English meaning, a realistic context, and a transfer prompt in the practice player.

## B2 strategy extension

The additional 100 B2 lessons are available in the app as a dedicated B2 filter and a three-item strategy sprint. They cover ten high-value domains: argumentation, meeting facilitation, negotiation, formal communication, analytical reasoning, project work, relationships, technology, society, and reflection. Every item has an English meaning, a realistic context, and a transfer prompt in the practice player.
